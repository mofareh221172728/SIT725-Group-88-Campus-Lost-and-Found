How can we help?

Find answers about reporting, finding and managing lost and found items on campus.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use Campus Lost & Found?

Deakin students. Log in with your Deakin email address to report items and manage your reports. Browsing reports does not need a login.

How do I log in?

Open the Login page and enter your Deakin email address. Your session ends when you log out or close the browser.

How do I report a lost or found item?

Go to Create Report, choose Lost or Found, then fill in the title, category, description, campus location and date. The date cannot be in the future.

Can I add photos to my report?

Yes. You can add up to three photos. A clear photo helps the owner or finder recognise the item.

What is the difference between email contact and drop-off?

When you report a found item, choose how it will be returned. Email lets the owner contact you. Drop-off means you leave the item at a campus location, such as Campus Security, and the owner collects it there.

How do I search for my lost item?

Use Search & Filter to search by keyword and narrow the results by category, location and date. You can also browse all reports from the Main page.

Someone found my item. How do I get it back?

Open the report to see its details. It shows either the finder's email address or the location where you can collect the item.

How do I edit my report?

Open My Reports and select Edit on the report. Only active reports can be edited.

My item has been returned. What should I do?

Open My Reports and mark the report as resolved. It will no longer appear in Browse or Search.
